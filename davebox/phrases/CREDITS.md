# Phrase library — credits

Some of the drum phrases in `phrases-open.pack` are adapted from these datasets, both
released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):

- **Groove MIDI Dataset** — Jon Gillick, Adam Roberts, Jesse Engel, Mark Johnson,
  Ricardo Garcia, Anna Huang and others, Google Magenta.
  https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/groove
  *Changes:* single bars of one instrument (kick, snare, hats, toms or ride) taken from
  the drummers' beat recordings, placed on a 16th-note grid, velocities kept as played.

- **Lakh MIDI Dataset** — Colin Raffel, *Learning-Based Methods for Comparing Sequences,
  with Applications to Audio-to-MIDI Alignment and Matching*, PhD thesis, 2016.
  https://colinraffel.com/projects/lmd/
  *Changes:* repeating one- and two-bar drum loops of one instrument taken from the
  LMD-matched files, placed on a 16th-note grid, velocities kept.

Those phrases keep these terms: you may use, change and share them, with this credit.
