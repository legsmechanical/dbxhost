# BREAKS — melodic (breakbeat, big beat, nu-skool, UK/acid breaks)

Conventions: `melodic/basics.md` § Conventions. `[Sn]` = `melodic/SOURCES.md`; `D5` = Chordonomicon
(91 songs tagged breakbeat/big beat/nu skool breaks). **(proposal)** = ours. ⚠ **Low confidence**: the
genre is defined by its drums; melodic sources describe sounds, not notes.

## Tempo, mode

- Umbrella **110–175 BPM** [S106]; big beat 100–140 [S107]; nu skool 125–140 [S108]; Bicep-style 128,
  "Swing: NONE" [S109]. Default 128–136 (nu-skool), 100–120 (big beat).
- Minor / modal: acid-house lineage [S107][S106]; D5: 23 % minor-proxy, **52 %** of minor songs use the
  major IV (Dorian — highest of the electronic tags), sections with **2 distinct chords 19 %** (vamps;
  rock 10 %).

## BASS

- Big beat: "intense, distorted synthesizer basslines", "TB-303 synthesizer lines resembling those of
  acid house" [S107]; nu skool "a dominant bass line" [S108]; 303 resonant low-pass [S106]; Reese
  "a bit of glide between notes" ~200 ms, octave-below second oscillator [S110]; Bicep "four-note bass
  line" [S109].
- **Relation to the kick (proposal)**: the breakbeat kick is syncopated (parent drum research), so the bass
  hits step 1 and the kick's 16th-pushes (e.g. 11 or 8), with rests under the snare (5, 13).
- Register (proposal): MIDI 28–45 (sub/Reese), acid one octave higher.


## CHORD (rave stabs)

- Rave stab: "the chord shape remains consistent as it's played up and down the keyboard" (parallel
  m7), "zero attack, full sustain and a short release tail", "harmonic and percussive drive in one go"
  [S111]; breakbeat = "ravey synths, stabs, glittery arps, and growling acid bass lines" (snippet [S112]).
- **(proposal)** parallel m7 stab, 1–4 hits per bar, off-beat 16ths, L 6–18 ticks, MIDI 55–74,
  vel 100–120.

```
KC1 parallel rave stab   x..x..x...x..x..   m7 shape on R, R, b7, b7, b6 (parallel)  [S111]
KC2 stab + answer        ......x.......x. | ......x.....x.x.   i7 short             (proposal)
```

## ARP

- "Glittery arps" (snippet [S112]); no note-level rules. **(proposal)** 16ths, 2 octaves, up, gate 30–40 %.

## LEAD

- Progressive breaks "melodic synth leads" [S106]; electro breaks "80s synths, robotic sounds … vocoders"
  [S106]. **(proposal)** reuse the electro/techno sparse-lead rules (2–5 notes / 2 bars).

## PAD

- Progressive breaks: "extended synthesizer pads and washes … heavy reverberation" [S106].
  **(proposal)** 1 chord per 2 bars, minor 7/9, width 12–24 st.

## NOT breaks

- Four-on-the-floor bass/stab implication (hits on all of 1/5/9/13) (proposal).
- Swung 16ths in the nu-skool family ("Swing: NONE" [S109]).
- Major-key bright pop progressions as the default (D5 mode lean + [S107] acid lineage).
