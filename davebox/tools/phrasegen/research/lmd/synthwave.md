# SYNTHWAVE — not measurable from LMD

**0 songs.** LMD's matched side is the Million Song Dataset, whose catalogue ends in 2011. No track in
LMD-matched carries a synthwave / retrowave / outrun / darksynth tag (Last.fm or Echo Nest), and the
genre's artists are absent: no Kavinsky, College, Perturbator, Carpenter Brut, Lazerhawk, Mitch Murder,
Gunship or The Midnight. The nearest things present are a handful of Italo-revival / dream-pop tracks
(Chromatics 1, Sally Shapiro 2, M83 5) — too few, and not synthwave.

`../drums/synthwave.md` and `../melodic/synthwave.md` therefore stay **guide-based**. What LMD *can*
say is how the genre's stated **80s ancestors** (synth-pop, Italo — `synthpop.md`, `italo.md`) were
played, which tests the guides' "imitates the 80s machines" premise:

| synthwave guide claim | ancestor measurement (synth-pop / Italo) | reading |
|---|---|---|
| Rock kick 1 + 9 (+ step 7), or 4otf | 1+9 47 % / 38 %; 4otf 32 % / 37 %; step-7 kick .29 / .24 | the two kick families **are** the ancestors' two families |
| Snare on 5/13 always | .79/.79 / .79/.82 | consistent |
| 16th hats **minus the backbeat** (no hat on 5/13) | hat P on 5/13 = on other 8ths (.69–.70 / .81) | **not an 80s trait** — a modern synthwave idiom; the ancestors keep the hat on every 8th |
| "Virtually no velocity deviation" (hat SD ≤ 6) | hat SD 9.6 / 11.2; kick SD 3.9 / 4.0 | kick/snare near-flat as claimed; **hats were not flat** in the ancestors |
| Open hat once per bar, step 15 | open-hat P peaks on 15 (.19 / .18) | consistent |
| Tom fills in bar 2 on 9–16, ≤ 30 % | fill bars 7 % / 6 %; toms in groove bars 4 % | consistent (fills are rare) |
| No swing | 0 % / 4 % of songs ≥ 4 ticks | consistent |
| Octave bass (lineage from Hi-NRG/Italo) | ≥ 25 % octave moves in 19 % / 20 % of songs; constant-pulse bass 34 % / 23 % | the octave-pulse bass is a **minority** in the ancestors too |
| Tempo bands 80–118 and 128–140 | ancestors' folded medians 126 / 123 | the slow (80–100) band has no 80s ancestor here |

To measure synthwave itself, a post-2011 MIDI source would be needed (none openly licensed is known to
us; it was not searched in this session).
