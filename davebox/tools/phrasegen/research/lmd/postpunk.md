# POST-PUNK / GOTH — measured (LMD)

**44 songs measured, 32 artists** — ⚠ **below the 50-song target**, thin and skewed. The set is The
Cure, Sisters of Mercy, New Order, Psychedelic Furs, Echo & the Bunnymen, The Smiths, Teardrop
Explodes, Stranglers, The Sound, The Church, Devo, Fad Gadget, plus minor goth/darkwave acts (Mantus,
Estigma, Sopor Aeternus, Miranda Sex Garden, Rosetta Stone, 45 Grave, The Mission). **Joy Division,
Siouxsie and the Banshees, Bauhaus and Killing Joke are not in LMD-matched**, so the guide's "tribal
toms" (Banshees/Budgie) and "dirge / bossa" (Bauhaus) families **cannot be measured** here. Error bars
are ± ~10–15 points on any share. Method/licences: `README.md`. Guide files compared:
`../drums/postpunk.md`, `../melodic/postpunk.md`.

## What the data says

- **Tempo:** file median 126 BPM (IQR 119–137); folded median 132 (122–144) — inside the guide's
  motorik / machine-goth 120–150. No swing (0 % of songs ≥ 4 ticks).
- **Kick:** 1 at .94, 9 at .78; 5/13 only .20/.17 (between rock .13 and new wave .33). 4otf 14 %,
  rock 1+9 kick 59 %. **Step 7 (.42) is the strongest off-beat kick of any genre measured** (rock .33,
  new wave .29), with 15 at .29: the `x.....x.x.....x.` family. Even 16ths ≈ 0.
- **Snare:** backbeat .75/.78 at vel 107–108 (backbeat songs 66 %), no ghosts. Half-time 2 %.
- **Hat:** 8ths .61–.66 on odd steps, .14–.20 on even steps; hat SD 15.6 (**the most dynamic hat
  of any genre**), tiering +9.1. **20 % of songs have no hat** (the most of any genre; new wave 8 %,
  Italo 5 %). 16th-hat songs 9 %. Open hat 12 % of hat hits, mostly on 15.
- **Perc:** in 55 % of songs, and **tambourine is 40 % of perc notes** (the most of any genre); the modal
  perc bar is 8ths (`x.x.x.x.x.x.x.x.`, 20 % of songs) with a lift on 5/13 (.45/.47).
- **Toms:** toms in groove bars in 7 % of songs (rock 3 %, new wave 4 %) — the most of any genre, but
  still a minority. **Cymbals:** crash/ride in 28 % of groove bars (rock 38 %); crash on 1 after a fill
  .60, after a groove bar .14.
- **Velocity:** 30 % of files flat (the most of any genre); kick ≤ 2 distinct velocities in 55 % of
  songs.
- **Bass (43 songs):** 4.8 notes/bar, 100 % on 8th positions, the **longest notes** of the targets
  (median 2.0 steps, gate 0.89; 21 % of notes ≥ 4 steps). Constant 8th pulse in 14 % of songs, 16th in
  5 %. Octave moves rare (9 % of songs ≥ 25 %). **Register is LOW:** p10/50/p90 32/37/41; only ~8 % of
  songs have a median bass note ≥ MIDI 47. Root share ~.27 (same as rock).
- **Harmony (weak):** 36 % minor. Minor songs: bVII .81, bIII .62, bVI .56 — power chords dominate
  their windows (39 %).
- **Other parts:** guitar in 89 % of songs; chord parts 77 % (power-chord windows 45 %); pads 61 %
  (strings ensemble 25 of 54); arps 7 %; organ in 32 % of songs (the most of the targets).
