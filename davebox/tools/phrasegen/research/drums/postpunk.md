# POST-PUNK / GOTH — drums

Conventions as in `techno.md`. **Guide-based.** The sources are artist and record descriptions
[K135–K145]. They are strong on *roles* (toms instead of kit, motorik, drum machines) but weak on
step grids and tempos: **no sourced BPM was found**.

Measured support is indirect:

- GMD tom-ostinato bars (DK3).
- GMD punk (`punk.md`, one drummer).
- `synthpop.md` for the drum-machine goth family.

**(proposal)** marks our own numbers, not a source's. Confidence is the lowest of the 80s set on
exact positions.

## Tempo and swing

- No sourced tempo.
  - **(proposal)** Motorik/Joy Division family 120–150; tribal/Banshees 110–140; dirge (Bauhaus)
    70–100; drum-machine goth (Sisters) 120–140.
- **Swing:** none.
  - Stephen Morris is noted for "'machine-like' skills … which he credits to krautrock" [K140].
  - Motorik is "repeated in each bar" [K142].
  - **(proposal)** Delay 0; jitter ≤ ±2.

## The four families

| family | defining trait | source |
|---|---|---|
| **motorik** | steady 4/4, "repeated in each bar", crash only at section starts | [K142][K140][K141] |
| **tribal toms** | "played mostly toms … the sound of cymbals was forbidden"; "a lot of tom-toms and a big throb" | [K135][K136][K137] |
| **dirge / dub** | "hypnotically dirgelike" beats; echo and delay on drums; Bauhaus built on "a bossa nova style" | [K137][K138][K139] |
| **drum-machine goth** | DR-55 → TR-606 → TR-808 / 909 → DMX (Doktor Avalanche); machines "downplaying the rhythm's backbeat" | [K144][K137] |

  "Steady drumming" [K145]. The kick's 8th-note drive is our reading of the motorik pattern
  **(proposal)**.

## SNARE

- **Weak backbeat:** "several acts used drum machines downplaying the rhythm's backbeat" [K137].
  - **(proposal)** The snare on 5/13 is present in the motorik and machine families but at
    *normal* level (90–105, not accented above the kick).
  - It is absent or replaced by a tom in the tribal family, in ≥ 50 % of tribal phrases.
- **"She's Lost Control":** "a mechanistic drum beat". Live, a Synare "would often be more abrasive"
  [K141].
  - **(proposal)** A Synare/electronic hit on 5/13 or 13 only, as a snare substitute.
- **Length (proposal):** 16–48 ticks. The flanger and echo are FX [K143][K138].

- **Sources:**
  - Siouxsie's Kenny Morris "played mostly toms", and "the sound of cymbals was forbidden" [K135].
  - Budgie used "a lot of tom-toms and a big throb" instead of "standard hi-hat–kick–snare", with a
    "tom-tom-intensive approach … enlightened by … world music" and "kinetic syncopation" [K136].
  - Gothic rock beats are "tom-tom heavy and 'tribal'" [K137].
- **Measured analogue:** DK3 found 818 GMD bars where toms play a repeated ostinato. Top patterns:
  - `x......xx......x` (a 3-3-2-style floor-tom groove);
  - tom 8ths `x.x.x.x.x.x.x.x.`;
  - `x...x.x.x...xxxx`.
  - While the toms play, the snare stays on 5/13 (P .61/.62).
  - ⚠ One rock file supplies 296 of the 818 bars, and the table mixes rock with Latin grooves. It
    is a shape reference, not a goth measurement.
- **(proposal)** Tribal family: the tom lane carries a 1-bar ostinato repeated exactly:
  - density 4–8 hits;
  - accents on 1 and 9 (vel 100–115), others 70–90;
  - ≥ 50 % of hits on 8th positions;
  - gate 24–48.
  - Bar 2 may add one 16th pickup (step 16).
  - In other families the tom lane is empty except for a bar-2 fill on 13–16.

## HAT

- Cymbals are "forbidden" in the Banshees family [K135]. **(proposal)** Empty in tribal phrases.
- **Motorik (proposal):** steady 8ths, flat two-level velocity.
- **Machine goth (proposal):** 606/808 closed 16ths or 8ths, short gate (3–6), one accent level.
  The DR-55 and TR-606 have one global accent [K144].
- **The Cure:** "seven flanger devices" on "A Forest" [K143] (an FX note, not a lane).

## PERC

- **Bauhaus:** "Bela Lugosi's Dead" "crackles in on percussion alone, a tapping, rattling rhythm"
  [K139], "based on a bossa nova style" [K138].
  - **(proposal)** A rim/clave lane with a bossa figure: 3-2 clave-like, e.g. steps 1, 4, 7, 11, 14.
  - Vel 70–95, gate 4–8, with echo as FX.
  - This is the dirge family's main lane; the kick is sparse (1 and 9).
- **Motorik / machine:** **(proposal)** Sparse or no perc.

## CYMB

- **Motorik:** "A splash or crash cymbal is often hit at the beginning bar of a verse or chorus"
  [K142]. → crash on step 1 of the first bar of a section only.
- **Tribal:** none [K135].
- **(proposal)** Never a ride. A crash in ≤ 1 bar per 8.

## 2-bar variations

- **(proposal)** Minimal. Motorik repeats exactly [K142].
- The tribal family may add one tom pickup.
- Machine goth may add a single open hat or a Synare hit on 15.

## NOT post-punk / goth

- Swing; funk ghost notes; busy rock fills.
- A ride or crash in most bars (cymbals forbidden or sparse [K135][K142]).
- An accented pop backbeat over a tribal tom figure [K137].
- Disco open hats in the tribal family.
