# EBM (Electronic Body Music) — drums

Conventions as in `techno.md`. **Guide-based and thin.** Wikipedia's genre and band articles
[K159–K161] give roles and sounds. One tutorial gives a tempo [K162]; its grid is in screenshots
only. The Front 242 and Nitzer Ebb articles have no drum detail. **(proposal)** = our own numbers.
Confidence is low on exact positions.

## Tempo and swing

- **Tempo:** 126 BPM, Nitzer Ebb / Phase Fatale style [K162].
  - **(proposal)** Default 118–132.
- **Swing:** none. DAF's drums sit between "the relentlessly robotic (the 16-step sequences)" and
  "fairly simple and relatively unsyncopated patterns, but with simple variations that prevented
  them sounding robotic" [K160].
  - **(proposal)** Delay 0; jitter ±1–2 on the DAF (live-drummer) family only.

## KICK

- "EBM is based around an incessant quarter-note kick drum pattern" [K159]. A 909 kick on quarter
  notes [K162].
  - Grid: 1/5/9/13 **always**.
- **Alternatives:** "Typical EBM rhythms rely on the 4/4 disco beat or rock-oriented backbeats …
  with some minor syncopation" [K159].
  - **(proposal)** A rock-backbeat family: kick 1/9 plus ≤ 1 syncopated 8th, at ≤ 25 % weight.
- **Velocity (proposal):** 115–127 flat.
- **Length (proposal):** 12–24 ticks.

## SNARE / CLAP

- **Backbeat:** "often with a backbeat snare" [K159]. Steps 5/13 **often**.
- **Half-time:** a "sharp snare on beat 3" (snippet, lower authority) [K163]. **(proposal)** A
  half-time family (step 9) at ≤ 20 %.
- **Velocity (proposal):** 105–120 flat.
- **Length (proposal):** 12–24 ticks. EBM snares are "sharp".

## HAT

- "A simple hat / snare / cymbal loop" on a TR-606 [K162].
- **(proposal)** Closed 8ths or off-beat 8ths, short gate (3–6), two-level velocity (70/95).
  Sometimes absent: the 16th sequence carries the motion [K160].

## TOM

- No source. **(proposal)** Empty. At most a 1-beat fill on 13–16 once per 4+ bars.

## PERC (the metallic lane)

- "Drum sounds were often samples of drums, car crashes, door slamming or environmental percussion"
  [K159].
- Die Krupps' "Stahlophon (metallic percussion)" and "industry factory noises with metallic
  percussion" [K161].
- Front 242's Richard 23 joined as a "noise concept" percussionist (agent report; no drum detail).
- **(proposal)** Metal-hit lane:
  - 1–4 hits per bar, on off-8ths or on 13/15.
  - Vel 100–127, two-level. Gate 12–36.
  - Repeated exactly every bar, or answering in bar 2.

## CYMB

- A cymbal is part of the 606 loop [K162].
- **(proposal)** 606 cymbal on step 1 of every 4th bar, or none.

## 2-bar variations

- "Simple variations that prevented them sounding robotic" [K160].
- **(proposal)** ≤ 2 events differ between bars: a metal hit moved, or an extra snare on 16.

## NOT EBM

- Swing, ghost notes, open disco hats with house shuffle.
- Busy fills.
- Syncopated breakbeat kicks: syncopation is "minor" [K159].
- Soft, dynamic playing.
